TMJ App

Learn from people who have been through TMJ treatment, and share your own story to help the next person.

iPhone beta coming soon

Why I'm building this

Finding a TMJ treatment that works often means piecing together advice from doctors, dentists, physical therapists and forum threads. TMJ App puts real treatment journeys in one place, so you can see what others tried, what helped, and what didn't.

What you can do

Read journeys

Browse other people's profiles and treatment notes. No account needed.

Share your story

Sign in to post your own journey and help others who are just getting started.

TMJ App

See what helps your TMJ, and what makes it worse.

Log jaw pain, treatments and triggers in seconds. Your timeline shows how they line up over days and weeks, so you can spot patterns and give your dentist, doctor or physical therapist a clear history.

iPhone beta coming soon

What you can do

Built for the days your jaw won't let you forget

Pain

Log pain as it happens

Record how bad it is, how it feels, and where it hurts by tapping a jaw, head and neck diagram.

Treatment

Track what you try

Note night guards, physical therapy, injections and more, with check-in reminders a few days after you start.

Trigger

Spot possible triggers

Log things like clenching, chewing hard food, long calls or poor sleep, and link them to pain entries.

Timeline

See it all in one place

Review every entry in order and filter by pain, treatment or trigger, handy before a specialist appointment.

Learn

Understand your jaw

Short, plain-language articles on jaw anatomy, pain types, common diagnoses, treatments and imaging.

Journeys

Learn from others

Read how other people navigated TMJ treatment, and share your own story to help the next person.

Private by default

Your tracking stays on your phone

Pain, treatment and trigger entries are stored on your device. You don't need an account to track, browse or read.

You only sign in if you choose to share a story on Journeys, and you decide what goes in it.
